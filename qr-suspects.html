📋 Teacher's Notes — How to use these QR codes

Setup: Print this page and cut out each card. Stick one card on each desk or table group before the lesson. Each card links directly to that suspect's interrogation interface.

Suggested grouping: 3–4 students per suspect, or one suspect per student for individual work.
Task sequence:
1. Interrogate your suspect using Past Continuous & Past Perfect
2. Fill in the Detective Worksheet
3. Share your findings with the class
4. Complete the 9-question quiz together
